Four problems · one gets built first

On the bench

Each of these is a job we've watched people do by hand. None of them is finished — that's the point. Leave your email on the one that hurts in your business, and we'll build that one next and bring you in while it's still cheap to change.

Validating invoices.zionex.app

Supplier invoices, typed in twice

PDFs and phone photos land in an inbox. Someone opens each one and retypes the supplier, the date, every line, the VAT — into accounting software that already has a field for all of it. Six hours a week, and the typos surface at month end.

For: bookkeepers and finance leads at 10–200 person companies

In design leads.zionex.app

The lead that sat unread until Monday

A form submission arrives Friday at 17:40. Nobody sees it until Monday, and by then the buyer has already talked to two competitors. The fix isn't a bigger CRM — it's something that answers in three minutes and books the call.

For: agencies, installers and B2B services with a contact form

Exploring chase.zionex.app

Overdue invoices nobody wants to chase

Chasing late payers is awkward, so it slips. A polite, well-timed sequence that escalates on its own recovers cash that's already yours — without anyone having to write the uncomfortable email.

For: owner-operators and finance teams carrying 30+ day receivables

Exploring orders.zionex.app

Orders that arrive as free-text email

Regular customers order the way they always have: a few lines in an email, sometimes a photo of a handwritten list. Someone reads it, guesses the SKUs and keys it into the ERP. One misread line becomes a wrong delivery.

For: wholesalers and distributors taking orders by email

How we work

We only build one thing at a time, and we'd rather kill an idea in week three than maintain it for a year.

Watch the job being done

Half an hour on a call while you do the task the way you do it today. No slides, no discovery workshop. If the pain turns out to be small, we say so and stop here.

Two weeks to a working tool

Not a mockup — something that takes your real files and produces a real result, used by three companies at once so we're not designing for one opinion.

Keep it or kill it

If it doesn't save measurable hours, it doesn't get a subdomain. The ones that survive get a proper home and a price.

Small on purpose

Who's behind this

Zionex is an independent software studio. We've spent years building and running production systems, and the same thing kept showing up: the expensive part of a business is rarely the software it bought — it's the manual work wrapped around it.

So we work narrow. One problem, one tool, one group of people it's obviously for. Everything here is in progress, and this page will change as things ship or get dropped.
